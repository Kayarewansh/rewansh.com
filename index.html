Common Questions

Consultant FAQ.

What does a digital marketing consultant do? +
A digital marketing consultant audits your current marketing, builds a channel-by-channel growth strategy, and either executes it hands-on or guides your team through execution — across SEO, paid media, content, and automation.
How much does a digital marketing consultant cost? +
Engagements start at $349 for a strategy session and $549/month for full growth-partner retainers. Enterprise engagements are scoped individually.
How long does it take to see results? +
Paid media can produce results within weeks. Organic growth from SEO and content typically compounds over 3–6 months — which is why engagements combine both.
What's the difference between a consultant and an agency? +
With a consultant, you work directly with the person doing the strategy — no account managers, no recycled playbooks, no junior handoffs. Every recommendation comes from a real audit of your business.
Do you work with small businesses or only enterprises? +
Both. Engagement tiers are built to fit startups, D2C brands, and enterprise teams — the process is standardized, the strategy is customized.
Do you offer fractional CMO services? +
Yes — fractional CMO engagements cover strategic leadership, channel prioritization, team guidance, and martech stack decisions without the cost of a full-time hire.
Do you work internationally as well as in India? +
Yes. Engagements run fully remote across India, the United States, United Kingdom, UAE, Germany, and worldwide.
Do you help with martech stack audits? +
Yes — stack audits identify overlapping tools and disconnected data, then consolidate your CRM, email platform, analytics, and automation into one integrated system.
